The verdict

Total EP sets your rarity tier and unlocks any badges you hit.

EP measures surprise, not size. A flopped royal flush earns far more than a rivered one — not because it's a bigger hand, but because it's astronomically rarer to get there early. No hand-tuning: the math does it for you.
