Privacy

What we collect: basically nothing, so far

Freeroll doesn't have accounts, a database, or a server yet — right now this is a static, informational site. Here's exactly what happens today.

Where this page stands: everything below describes freeroll.org as it actually runs right now, not the eventual game. It'll be rewritten to match reality before anything below stops being true — not after.

What we store

Your theme choice (light, dark, or auto) is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key freeroll-theme. It never leaves your device — we don't receive it, see it, or store a copy of it ourselves. Clearing your browser data clears it.

That's the only thing this site writes anywhere. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no email collection, no cookies set by us, and no analytics or tracking scripts embedded on any page.

Hosting

Freeroll is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any website, Cloudflare processes standard connection metadata (such as your IP address) as part of serving the page and protecting it from abuse — that's Cloudflare acting as our infrastructure provider, not data we separately collect or can see ourselves. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle it.

When the actual game ships

The plan is passwordless sign-in, a chosen nickname, and a record of your daily deals and leaderboard results — modeled closely on how rngdle.com handles it, including hashing rather than storing anything sensitive in the clear. None of that exists yet. When it does, this page changes first.
